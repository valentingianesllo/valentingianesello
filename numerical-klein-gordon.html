01 — BACKGROUND

Context

This numerical project was carried out as part of my preparation for relativistic quantum mechanics. Its aim was to explore the Klein-Gordon equation as an introduction to relativistic quantum theory, while providing a first step towards understanding the Dirac equation. It also offered an opportunity to apply and further develop my computational physics skills through the numerical study and simulation of relativistic quantum phenomena.
