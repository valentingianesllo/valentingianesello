01 — BACKGROUND

Context

This project was carried out in collaboration with a fellow student from my academic cohort, under the supervision of Professor Jean-Marc Di Meglio at Université Paris Cité. The aim was to investigate classical problems in mechanical stability, approaching them through mathematical modelling and rigorous analysis. Working on this project alongside a fellow student was a particularly enjoyable experience, allowing us to explore these problems together and develop a deeper understanding of the mathematical principles governing mechanical equilibrium and stability.

02 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Content

This project revisits a collection of historically significant problems in mechanics, reformulating them within a rigorous mathematical framework. It explores questions involving the stacking of bricks, including the famous problem of constructing an arbitrarily large overhang using a finite number of blocks, as well as the equilibrium and stability of arches and other mechanical structures. Each problem is approached through mathematical modelling, with particular attention paid to equilibrium conditions, force distributions, constraints and stability criteria. The project aims to provide precise mathematical formulations of these classical problems and to investigate the conditions under which seemingly unstable structures can remain in mechanical equilibrium.
