01 — BACKGROUND

Context

This work was carried out during an academic internship in my second year of undergraduate studies (L2), under the supervision of Paul LeBreton, one of my mathematics professors and a PhD candidate specialising in differential geometry, particularly in the study of partial differential equations. The aim of this project was to develop a rigorous mathematical introduction to differential geometry, exploring the geometric tools that play a fundamental role in modern theoretical physics.

02 — BOOK OVERVIEW

Content

This book provides a comprehensive introduction to differential geometry, with the aim of developing the mathematical tools necessary to understand differentiable manifolds and the mathematical foundations of General Relativity. The presentation is rigorous and includes detailed derivations and mathematical proofs, progressively introducing the concepts and structures underlying Einstein's theory of gravitation. It also features numerous exercises exploring both the mathematical formalism and its physical applications, including spacetime metrics, black holes, wormholes and other topics related to General Relativity.
